# GHSG Time Clock - Manager Handbook

## Manager Dashboard Guide

"Developing leaders, not just filling shifts."

As a site manager, you are responsible for approving timesheets, monitoring your team, and ensuring accurate payroll data.

---

## Accessing the Manager Dashboard

1. Log in with your Employee ID or Google account
2. Tap **"Approvals"** in the navigation menu
3. You'll see two tabs: **Pending** and **Team Status**

---

## Approving Timesheets

### Viewing Pending Approvals

1. Go to **Approvals** → **Pending** tab
2. You'll see all timesheets waiting for your review
3. Each card shows:
   - Employee name and photo
   - Site name
   - Week dates
   - Total hours
   - Number of entries

### Reviewing a Timesheet

1. Tap **"View"** to see full details
2. Review:
   - Daily hours breakdown
   - Clock in/out times
   - Lunch breaks taken
   - Location verification status
   - Photo verification

### Approving or Rejecting

**To Approve:**
1. Review all entries
2. Add any notes (optional)
3. Tap **"Approve Timesheet"**

**To Reject:**
1. Add a note explaining why
2. Tap **"Reject Timesheet"**
3. Employee will be notified to correct and resubmit

---

## Approval Links

You can also approve timesheets via email/text links:

1. When an employee submits a timesheet, you'll receive a notification
2. Click the approval link to open directly in your browser
3. Review and approve without logging into the full app

---

## Monitoring Team Status

### Real-Time Status

1. Go to **Approvals** → **Team Status** tab
2. See which employees are:
   - 🟢 **Active** - Currently clocked in
   - ⚪ **Off** - Not clocked in
3. View their clock-in time and location status

---

## Making Corrections

If an employee made an error:

1. Open the timesheet
2. Add corrections in the notes
3. Approve with corrections noted
4. Or reject and have employee fix and resubmit

---

## Exporting Reports

### Weekly Site Report

1. Go to **Admin** → **Reports** (if you have access)
2. Select your site and week
3. Export as CSV for payroll

### Audit Trail

For compliance records:
1. Export audit trail showing all approvals
2. Includes lunch break verification
3. Keep for records as required

---

## Best Practices

✅ **Review timesheets daily** - Don't wait until week end
✅ **Check location verification** - Flag any consistently off-site entries
✅ **Verify lunch breaks** - Required for compliance
✅ **Add notes** - Document any corrections or issues
✅ **Approve promptly** - Payroll depends on timely approvals

---

## Common Situations

**Employee forgot to clock out:**
- They should notify you immediately
- You can note this in the timesheet approval
- Track patterns of forgotten punches

**Employee clocked in off-site:**
- Check if there's a valid reason
- Note in approval if acceptable
- Address pattern issues with employee

**Disputed hours:**
- Review photo timestamps
- Check location data
- Document resolution in notes
